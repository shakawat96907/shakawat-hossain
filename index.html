About Md. Shakawat Hossain

I am a final-year Biochemistry and Molecular Biology student and an active Cancer Researcher passionate about unraveling the molecular complexities of cancer and translating genomic insights into precision medicine solutions.

My research sits at the intersection of bioinformatics, transcriptomics, and precision oncology. I leverage computational tools to analyze high-throughput sequencing data, identify therapeutic targets, and contribute to the development of personalized cancer treatment strategies. With a strong foundation in molecular biology and advanced computational skills, I aim to bridge the gap between bench research and clinical application.
